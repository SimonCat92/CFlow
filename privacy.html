Privacy Notice

The short version

This website is a collection of static files that run entirely in your browser. It processes no personal data itself. There is no backend server or database and no accounts. The site uses no cookies, no storage of any kind (local or session storage, browser databases, Cache Storage, service workers) and no analytics or tracking.

The privacy banner appears on every visit because the site keeps no record that you have already seen it.

What happens when you press “Compile”

When you press Compile, your browser opens a direct connection to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) and sends:

  • your Cƒ program (the text in the editor),
  • the Cƒ ruleset (rules.cf), and
  • the OpenRouter API key you typed, as the authorization credential.

This request goes directly from your browser to OpenRouter. The site has no server that could see, relay, log or store any of it. Your API key stays in the page's memory, reaches openrouter.ai only as the request's authorization header, and disappears when you close or reload the tab or press “Clear key”.

Requests ask OpenRouter to route only to Zero-Data-Retention endpoints (provider.zdr = true) and deny data collection (data_collection: "deny"). Your use of OpenRouter is governed by your own account, OpenRouter's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and the policies of the underlying model provider that serves your request. You pay for the usage under your own account.
